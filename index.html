Exploring cooperation mechanisms via reinforcement learning in network common-pool resource games

Yihang Qin, Lin Wang

arXiv preprint arXiv:2606.05867 2026

Used GNN-based reinforcement learning agent to design adaptive allocation mechanisms for sustaining cooperation in network common-pool resource games.

arXiv

Overview of the network common-pool resource game.

This work studies how reinforcement learning can be used to promote cooperation in networked common-pool resource games. I developed a resource-constrained evolutionary game model, designed a learnable allocation mechanism, and compared the learned policy with classical allocation rules. The results show that adaptive resource allocation can improve cooperation, reduce resource inequality, and enhance the sustainability of the system.

Recovering cooperation in Spatial Prisoner’s Dilemma via a forced loner mechanism

Yihang Qin, Hao Chen, Lin Wang

Physica A: Statistical Mechanics and its Applications 2026

Inspired by social welfare safety nets, this work develops a Forced Loner Mechanism to recover cooperation and improve efficiency, sustainability, and fairness in resource-constrained spatial Prisoner’s Dilemma games.

DOI

Forced Loner Mechanism (FLM) overview.

In this work, I study how a forced exit-and-support mechanism can recover cooperation in a resource-constrained Spatial Prisoner’s Dilemma. When agents become bankrupt, they are forced to become loners and receive a small subsistence payoff, representing social subsidies or institutional safety nets. The results show that this mechanism can suppress defector dominance, restore cooperation, and improve system-level efficiency, sustainability, and fairness.
