About me

Hi, I'm Yihang Qin, currently pursuing a master's degree at the Complex Networks and Control Lab of Shanghai Jiao Tong University, advised by Prof. Lin Wang. Prior to this, I obtained my B.Eng. in Automation from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen in 2024, where I was advised by Prof. Zilu Wang.

My research focuses on cooperation, artificial intelligence, and evolutionary game theory. I am especially interested in understanding how AI can promote cooperation in human and multi-agent environments currently.

I hope my research can contribute to a deeper scientific understanding of cooperation in human society. If you think we share similar interests, feel free to reach out for discussion~

Here is my CV. I am currently seeking Ph.D. opportunities in cooperation, artificial intelligence, evolutionary game theory, and human-AI interaction.
