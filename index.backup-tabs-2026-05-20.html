About Me

About Me / 关于我

Hi, I'm Yihang Qin. My current work centers on how artificial intelligence can promote cooperation, especially through deep reinforcement learning and evolutionary game theory.

I am particularly interested in combining formal modeling, simulation, and behavior-oriented experiments to understand how strategic interaction evolves in complex systems. If our interests overlap, I would be happy to connect and discuss ideas.

你好，我是秦一航。目前我的研究主要围绕人工智能如何促进合作展开，尤其关注深度强化学习与演化博弈理论。

我希望把形式化建模、仿真分析与面向真实行为的实验研究结合起来，理解复杂系统中的策略互动如何演化。如果你也对这些问题感兴趣，欢迎随时联系我交流。
